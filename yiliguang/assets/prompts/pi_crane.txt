The two separate parts of one jointed shadow puppet of a red-crowned crane in flight, a traditional Chinese shadow puppet (皮影) piece of carved and dyed translucent donkey hide, in exactly the same style as the reference pieces: rich translucent reds, greens, yellows and amber, every surface cut through with intricate fine openwork patterns (scales, flowers, dots, scrolls) so light shines through, thick dark cut edges, laid out apart with clear empty space between them, the same scale: (1) the crane's body seen from the side facing RIGHT, with its long neck stretched forward, the red crown, the long legs trailing behind, but WITHOUT wings; (2) one long wing on its own, spread, with black-tipped flight feathers. White hide with black and red details. Fully transparent background, nothing else, no text.
