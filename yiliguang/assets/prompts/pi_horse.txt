The separate parts of one jointed shadow puppet of a war horse, a traditional Chinese shadow puppet (皮影) piece of carved and dyed translucent donkey hide, in exactly the same style as the reference pieces: rich translucent reds, greens, yellows and amber, every surface cut through with intricate fine openwork patterns (scales, flowers, dots, scrolls) so light shines through, thick dark cut edges. Laid out apart from each other with clear empty space between them, all at the same scale: (1) the horse's body seen from the side facing RIGHT, with its head, arched neck, flowing mane, saddle cloth and tail, but WITHOUT any legs (the leg joints are round rivet holes at the shoulder and the haunch); (2) one front leg, straight, with its hoof; (3) one hind leg, bent at the hock, with its hoof. Fully transparent background, nothing else, no text.
