Traditional Chinese ink wash painting (水墨画), black ink only on plain pure white paper, expressive brushwork: wet ink with soft bleeding edges, dry-brush strokes, varied ink tones from pale grey to deep black. Nothing else in the picture: no paper texture, no frame, no seal, no text, no signature, pure white everywhere else.
Only a band of reeds, rushes and grasses rising from the bottom edge of the picture along its whole width, painted large and close, as if just in front of the viewer at the water's edge: tall thin blades and stems leaning a little to the right in the wind, some with feathery reed heads, in strong dark strokes and a few pale ones, some soft and blurred. They reach up to about a third of the picture's height; above them everything is empty white.
