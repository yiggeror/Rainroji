This is a sprite sheet for frame-by-frame hand-drawn animation: six frames of one continuous cycle, side by side in a single row, in six equal columns. One figure per column, centred in it, never touching the neighbouring columns. It is the very same figure in every frame, at exactly the same size and scale, with its feet (or lowest point) on the same baseline near the bottom of the picture; only the moving parts change from frame to frame. Seen exactly from the side. Fully transparent background, nothing else in the picture: no ground, no shadow, no rain, no text, no numbers, no frame lines.

The figure: a cab horse in harness (blinkers, collar, the two shafts of the cab ending just behind it, no cab), trotting to the LEFT, head a little down, drawn in black Conté crayon on white laid paper in exactly the same technique as the reference drawing (Georges Seurat's tonal Conté crayon drawings): no outlines at all, only soft grainy tonal masses built up on the tooth of the paper, velvety blacks, greys made of tiny specks, edges that dissolve softly. The six frames are one full trot cycle, diagonal legs moving together: 1) right front and left hind legs reaching forward; 2) those legs down, body lowest; 3) legs passing under; 4) left front and right hind reaching forward; 5) down; 6) passing. The mane and tail swing. The horse is the same size and in the same place in each column.
