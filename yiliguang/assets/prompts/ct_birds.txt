This is a sprite sheet for frame-by-frame hand-drawn animation: six frames of one continuous cycle, side by side in a single row, in six equal columns. One figure per column, centred in it, never touching the neighbouring columns. It is the very same figure in every frame, at exactly the same size and scale, with its feet (or lowest point) on the same baseline near the bottom of the picture; only the moving parts change from frame to frame. Seen exactly from the side. Fully transparent background, nothing else in the picture: no ground, no shadow, no rain, no text, no numbers, no frame lines.

The figure: one pigeon in flight, seen from the side flying to the right, drawn in black Conté crayon on white laid paper in exactly the same technique as the reference drawing (Georges Seurat's tonal Conté crayon drawings): no outlines at all, only soft grainy tonal masses built up on the tooth of the paper, velvety blacks, greys made of tiny specks, edges that dissolve softly. The six frames are one full wing-beat: 1) wings fully up; 2) wings halfway down; 3) wings fully down; 4) wings sweeping back and up, folded a little; 5) wings rising; 6) wings almost fully up. The body stays level and in the same place in each column.
