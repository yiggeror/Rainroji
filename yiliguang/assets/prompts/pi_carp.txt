The two separate parts of one jointed shadow puppet of a leaping golden carp, a traditional Chinese shadow puppet (皮影) piece of carved and dyed translucent donkey hide, in exactly the same style as the reference pieces: rich translucent reds, greens, yellows and amber, every surface cut through with intricate fine openwork patterns (scales, flowers, dots, scrolls) so light shines through, thick dark cut edges, laid out apart with clear empty space between them, the same scale: (1) the carp's body seen from the side facing RIGHT, head, gills, scales and fins, but WITHOUT its tail fin (ending in a round rivet hole where the tail joins); (2) the carp's big flowing forked tail fin on its own. Gold, orange and red hide. Fully transparent background, nothing else, no text.
