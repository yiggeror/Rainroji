A very wide, continuous horizontal panorama of a midsummer afternoon sky, painted by hand as background art for an animated film: opaque gouache / poster colour on paper, confident flat brushwork with crisp painted edges, rich but clean colour. Sky only: there is no land, no ground, no horizon objects at all; the bottom edge of the picture is the hazy horizon sky itself.
Colour: at the top a deep saturated cerulean-cobalt blue, almost violet at the very top; it pales steadily downward to a warm, sun-bleached pale aqua-white haze along the bottom edge.
Light: the afternoon sun is high and to the upper left, just out of the picture; the sky is a little brighter and warmer in the upper left.

Plenty of open blue sky between them.
