A long horizontal strip of handmade stop-motion sea for a tabletop set, in exactly the materials and lighting of the reference photo: medium waves of crumpled blue, turquoise and violet cellophane folded into peaks, with folded white tissue-paper whitecaps and tufts of white wool foam, bright glints where the light catches the cellophane.
