A single frame from a hand-painted animated film, 16:9. Geometric abstraction in the manner of Wassily Kandinsky's 1920s Bauhaus-period paintings: on a warm pale ground, precise circles, arcs, triangles, thin ruled lines, small checkerboard strips and grids, all in flat colour — ultramarine, cadmium yellow, vermilion, black, lilac, soft green — some circles ringed with soft halos. The composition still remembers a water-lily pond it has just become: large flat circles that were lily pads, a few green arcs that were willow fronds, a faint horizon line. Left of centre, one tiny point of warm golden light glows, brighter than everything else; several thin lines radiate from it like the first notes of a score. Visible canvas weave and slightly uneven oil-paint edges. No text, no signature, no picture frame.
