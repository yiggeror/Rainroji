One shadow puppet figure of an old fisherman in a straw rain cape and a wide conical bamboo hat, standing and leaning forward, holding a long thin fishing rod out in front of him with the line hanging down, a traditional Chinese shadow puppet (皮影) of carved and dyed translucent donkey hide, in exactly the same style, colours and carving as the reference pieces: rich translucent reds, greens and golden yellows, fine openwork patterns of scales, flowers and scrolls, every piece outlined with a thin dark edge and pierced with tiny holes. Seen from the side, facing right. Fully transparent background, flat and seen straight on, no shadow, no text.
