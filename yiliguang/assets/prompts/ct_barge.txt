One single object on its own, drawn in black Conté crayon on white laid paper in exactly the same technique as the reference drawing (Georges Seurat's tonal Conté crayon drawings): no outlines at all, only soft grainy tonal masses built up on the tooth of the paper, velvety blacks, greys made of tiny specks, edges that dissolve softly: a long low river barge seen exactly from the side, facing LEFT, a small cabin at the back with a stovepipe chimney and a lit window glowing, a lantern hanging from a short pole at the front glowing (paper white), a boatman in a cape standing at the back holding a long steering oar, a few barrels and sacks on deck. The barge fills most of the picture's width; nothing else: no water, no reflection, no smoke. Fully transparent background, no text.
