Traditional Chinese shadow-puppet (piying) pieces, shown exactly as they look lit from behind against a glowing screen: cut from thin translucent dyed hide, the colours glowing like stained glass — vermilion red, emerald green, jade teal, amber yellow, with some black; covered in dense, delicate openwork carving (lattice, scrolling lines, rows of dots, whorls, scales, petal shapes) through which bright white light shows; thin dark brown cut edges. Perfectly flat and seen exactly from the side, no perspective, no shading, no shadows, no rods or sticks, no strings. Fully transparent background around every piece.
One single wide piece: a long stretch of city wall with crenellations, and in its middle a two-storey gate tower with upturned eaves above an arched gateway; banners on the wall. It stands on the bottom edge and spans almost the whole width. No text, no border.
