One old cast-iron gas street lamp on its tall post, drawn in black Conté crayon on paper in exactly the technique of the reference drawing (Seurat-like grainy tonal masses, no outlines): the post a soft dark vertical, the lantern head a darker cage with its glass left as bare white paper. Only the lamp and post, isolated on a fully transparent background, no halo, no ground, no shadow. Tall vertical composition, the post reaching the bottom edge.
