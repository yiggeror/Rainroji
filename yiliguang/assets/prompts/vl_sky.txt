A very wide, continuous horizontal panorama for the background of an animated film, made as a Japanese shin-hanga colour woodblock print (early twentieth-century style): flat areas of colour printed from carved blocks, crisp carved key-block outlines, smooth bokashi gradations, the grain of the wood showing in the sky, faint baren rubbing marks, soft fibrous washi paper.
Subject: a still winter night, deep in snow, far from any town. No moon, no stars, no falling snow.
Layout (important): the sky fills the upper two thirds. Across the whole width runs a range of snow-covered mountains whose ridges stay low: the highest peak, a broad snowy pyramid exactly in the centre, reaches only about 27% of the height from the bottom edge; the other peaks are lower (12–20% of the height), overlapping in two or three ranges, the farther ones paler and bluer. At their feet, along the bottom edge (the lowest 6–8% of the height), dark forests of pines and cedars in deep indigo, their pointed tops forming a serrated line.
Colour: the sky is a bokashi gradation from deep Prussian-blue indigo at the top to a soft luminous grey-blue just above the mountains, the glow of snow-light. The mountains' snow faces turned to the left are pale blue-white, their shaded faces slate blue and violet-grey, with carved gullies and ridgelines; thin mist lies between the ranges.
