One single shadow puppet figure, a traditional Chinese shadow puppet (皮影) piece of carved and dyed translucent donkey hide, in exactly the same style as the reference pieces: rich translucent reds, greens, yellows and amber, every surface cut through with intricate fine openwork patterns (scales, flowers, dots, scrolls) so light shines through, thick dark cut edges: a general in ornate armour sitting astride (as if on a horse, but no horse), seen from the side facing RIGHT, holding the reins forward, a helmet crowned with two long sweeping pheasant feathers curving back, a cape flying behind, a small pennant on a pole on his back. Fully transparent background, nothing else, no text.
