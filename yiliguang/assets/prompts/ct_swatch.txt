A flat scan of a tonal study drawn with black Conté crayon on coarse white laid paper: a smooth horizontal gradient from bare white paper on the left to the deepest velvety black on the right, built only by the crayon dragged lightly across the paper's tooth, so every grey is made of tiny grainy specks in exactly the technique of the reference drawing. Even, uniform from top to bottom, no strokes visible as lines, no objects, no edges of the sheet, fills the whole frame.
