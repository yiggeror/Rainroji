Traditional Chinese ink wash painting (水墨画), black ink only on plain pure white paper, expressive brushwork: wet ink with soft bleeding edges, dry-brush strokes, varied ink tones from pale grey to deep black. Nothing else in the picture: no paper texture, no frame, no seal, no text, no signature, pure white everywhere else.
Only one gnarled old pine branch reaching in from the top-left corner and across the upper part of the picture, painted large and close, as if just in front of the viewer: a rough twisting branch in dark dry-brush strokes, with several clusters of pine needles painted as radiating fans of fine dark strokes, a few needles soft and pale where they are further away. The branch enters from the left edge and the top edge; the rest of the picture is empty white.
