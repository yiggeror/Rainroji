A very wide, continuous horizontal panorama for the background of an animated film, drawn entirely in black Conté crayon on coarse white laid paper, in exactly the same technique as the two reference drawings (Georges Seurat's tonal drawings): no outlines, only soft grainy tonal masses built on the tooth of the paper, velvety blacks, glowing paper whites, every grey made of tiny specks. From left to right it is one place: on the left, a quiet street at night after rain — a row of old houses receding, one softly lit upper window, wet cobbles with long faint reflections; the street slopes down to the right and opens onto a riverbank; on the right, the calm pale band of the river, a stone bridge with several arches dissolving into haze, the far bank's dark rounded trees, mist over the water. Leave the foreground empty and simple: no lamp posts, no people, no animals, no large foreground trees (these will be added as separate layers). Horizon at about 45% of the height across the whole width. Strictly monochrome, no colour, no text, no signature, no border.
