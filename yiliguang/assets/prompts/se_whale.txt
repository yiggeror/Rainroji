A humpback whale seen exactly side-on, swimming towards the left, its whole body from the tip of the snout at the left to the tail flukes at the right, painted in loose, luminous transparent watercolour on white paper, on a fully transparent background (nothing around the whale).
The body is straight and horizontal and fills the width of the picture, with a small margin at each end; it is long and streamlined, about four and a half times as long as it is deep.
Its colouring: a dark slate blue-grey and indigo back laid wet-in-wet, with soft blooms, backruns and granulating ultramarine and a touch of violet and burnt sienna; the flank lifted lighter where light from above falls along it in a long pale streak; a pale grey-white throat and belly with long parallel throat grooves running from under the chin back to the middle of the body; a few pale knobs (tubercles) along the top of the head and the chin; a couple of small clusters of pale barnacles on the chin; faint white scars on the back half; a small dorsal fin two-thirds of the way back; the tail flukes at the right seen a little from above, dark on top, their trailing edges ragged.
The eye is small and dark, just above the corner of the long curving mouth line, about a quarter of the body length back from the tip of the snout.
The near pectoral flipper is NOT shown at all (it will be added separately): the side of the body under the eye is smooth and unbroken.
