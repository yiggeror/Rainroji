Traditional Chinese ink wash painting (水墨画), black ink only on plain pure white paper, expressive brushwork: wet ink with soft bleeding edges, dry-brush strokes, varied ink tones from pale grey to deep black. Nothing else in the picture: no paper texture, no frame, no seal, no text, no signature, pure white everywhere else.
A sheet of six drawings of the same swallow in flight, painted in a few quick confident strokes each (the way swallows are painted in Chinese ink painting): forked tail, dark back and wings, a touch of pale on the throat. The six drawings are the frames of one wingbeat, laid out evenly in one row from left to right, all the same size and all flying to the right: wings fully up, wings half up, wings level, wings half down, wings fully down, wings folded in a glide. Each drawing well separated from the others.
