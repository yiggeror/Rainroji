The separate parts of one jointed shadow puppet of a mounted general, made to sit astride the war horse of the second reference, a traditional Chinese shadow puppet (皮影) of carved and dyed translucent donkey hide, in exactly the same style, colours and carving as the references: rich translucent reds, greens and golden yellows, fine openwork patterns of scales, flowers and scrolls, every piece outlined with a thin dark edge and pierced with tiny holes. The same general as in the first reference (the same helmet with long pheasant plumes, the same face with a black beard, the same armour and cape), seen from the side, facing right. Three separate pieces laid out apart from one another on a plain background, not touching:
1) his body from the hips up: head, helmet and plumes, armour, the cape flying back behind him, sitting upright; it ends at the hips in a round joint;
2) his leg as it hangs astride a horse: thigh forward and down, knee bent, the boot in a stirrup, with a round joint at the hip end;
3) his right arm, raised forward, holding a long spear with a red tassel, with a round joint at the shoulder end.
Fully transparent background, the pieces flat and seen straight on, no shadow, no text.
