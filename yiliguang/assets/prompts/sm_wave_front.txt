A long horizontal strip of handmade stop-motion sea for a tabletop set, in exactly the materials and lighting of the reference photo: the nearest row of big waves, close to the macro lens, crumpled cellophane in deep blue, teal and a little purple with sharp glinting creases, white wool foam and tissue-paper spray on the tall crests, slightly soft focus from the shallow depth of field. The waves occupy only the bottom 55% of the frame; everything above the crests is fully transparent.
