This reference is a crop of a finished transparent-watercolour painting of a humpback whale's head, seen exactly side-on, on white paper. Repaint it as the very same painting seen at a higher resolution, as if photographed closer.

Keep everything exactly where it is and as it is: the outline of the head and the jaw, the long curved mouth line, the eye and its position, the row of rounded knobs along the top of the head, the two barnacle clusters on the chin, every throat groove, the light and dark patches of the skin, the colours (slate blue, blue-grey, violet-grey, the pale cream-white of the grooves) and the values. Do not move, add or remove anything, do not change the lighting, do not change the colours, do not crop or zoom.

Only add the fine detail a close look at the real painting would reveal: the granulation of the pigment and the cold-pressed paper's tooth, soft wet-in-wet blooms and back-runs inside the darker washes, fine skin creases and small wrinkles fanning out from the corners of the eye, a heavy upper lid fold, the eye wet and glossy with a clear small highlight, a dark pupil in the brown iris, the knobs each with a small bristle pit and soft shadow, individual small barnacles and shells in the clusters with their rough white edges, the crisp painted edges of the throat grooves with slightly darker lines between them, a few pale scratches and scars.

Plain white paper around the whale.
