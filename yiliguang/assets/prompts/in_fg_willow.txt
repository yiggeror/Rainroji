Traditional Chinese ink wash painting (水墨画), black ink only on plain pure white paper, expressive brushwork: wet ink with soft bleeding edges, dry-brush strokes, varied ink tones from pale grey to deep black. Nothing else in the picture: no paper texture, no frame, no seal, no text, no signature, pure white everywhere else.
Only long willow strands hanging down from the top edge of the picture, painted large and close, as if just in front of the viewer: a dozen long, thin, gently curving strands of small leaves in pale and mid grey ink, a few darker, hanging from the top edge down to about the middle of the picture, sparser at the ends. Everything below them is empty white.
