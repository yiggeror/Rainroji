# Credits

## Picture
Every frame is rendered by the code in this folder (a small ray tracer written for this film, procedural
materials, per-frame effects, palette quantisation). No image assets, no photographs, no image model.
Fonts used for the signs painted into the scenes: WenQuanYi Zen Hei (GPL v2 with font-embedding exception),
AR PL UKai / UMing (Arphic Public License).

## Music
**HoliznaCC0 - "Lullabies For The End Of The World #1"**, from the album *Lullabies For The End Of The World*.
Released under **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication).
<https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/lullabies-for-the-end-of-the-world/lullabies-for-the-end-of-the-world-1/>

The track is used as released (only levelled and faded out at the end). `audio/music/` also holds three other
CC0 tracks by the same artist that were shortlisted, so the music can be swapped by changing `MUSIC` in
`make_film.py`: "Never Sleeping ( Lofi , Dark , Sad )", "Before Everything ( LoFi , Nostalgic )" and
"Going Home", all from *Public Domain Lofi* (<https://freemusicarchive.org/music/holiznacc0/public-domain-lofi>).
