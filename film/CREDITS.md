# Credits

## Picture
Every frame is rendered by the code in this folder (a small ray tracer written for this film, procedural
materials, per-frame effects, palette quantisation). No image assets, no photographs, no image model.
Fonts used for the signs painted into the scenes: WenQuanYi Zen Hei (GPL v2 with font-embedding exception),
AR PL UKai / UMing (Arphic Public License).

## Music
Original, composed and rendered by `audio/music.py` for this film (General MIDI piano and music box from
FluidR3_GM.sf2, MIT licence, then slowed, wobbled, filtered and drowned in reverb). The mood follows the
reference the film was made after ("i was only temporary" by my head is empty); no part of that recording
is used.
